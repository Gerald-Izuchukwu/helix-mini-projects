4. What is the difference between the Pod you created earlier and the Pods created by the
Deployment?
The standalone nginx-pod is unmanaged so if it dies, nothing recreates it. 
The Deployment-managed pods are owned by a ReplicaSet, which continuously reconciles the actual pod count against the desired replicas: 3, 
recreating any pod that's deleted or crashes

5. Why would we use a Deployment instead of creating individual Pods manually?
- For declarative desired-state management: self-healing, easy scaling, rolling updates/rollbacks, and no manual tracking of individual pod names

5. Why is scaling a Deployment easier than manually creating additional Pods?
- one declarative command adjusts the desired count and the ReplicaSet handles creating/terminating the right number of pods,
  no manual pod-by-pod creation or deletion, no risk of naming collisions or forgetting to update anything
